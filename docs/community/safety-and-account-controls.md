Rotur provides MistWarp accounts and sign-in. Your Rotur username identifies you on MistWarp, but Rotur manages your credentials and account recovery.

## Mute and block

Open a MistWarp user profile to mute or block that user.

- Muting hides MistWarp notifications from that user. It does not unfollow them on Rotur.
- Blocking stops MistWarp comments and notifications between both users. It does not change either person's Rotur account.

You can review and remove blocks or mutes under **Settings**, then **Safety**.

## Reports and support

Use the report button on a project, profile, or comment when content breaks the community terms. Reports go to MistWarp moderators.

Use the [support page](https://mistwarp.org/support) for private account help, safety concerns, legal requests, and moderation appeals. Product bugs belong on the [Roadmap](https://mistwarp.org/roadmap), where other users can confirm them and follow their status.

## Export or delete MistWarp data

Open **Settings**, then **Your data** to download a JSON export or delete the data attached to your MistWarp profile.

Deleting MistWarp data signs you out, deletes your MistWarp projects, and removes MistWarp profile data. MistWarp anonymizes public comments when removing them would break a conversation. This does not delete your Rotur account. Signing in with Rotur again creates a fresh MistWarp profile.
