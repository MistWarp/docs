The Explore page finds projects by title, creator, and tag. It has four sort modes:

- **Trending** ranks projects by views from the past seven days.
- **Recent** shows newly shared projects first.
- **Most loved** ranks projects by hearts.
- **Undiscovered** starts with projects that have the fewest views.

Category buttons apply common tags. Select **Load more** to continue through the complete result set.

## Spaces

A space collects projects around a group or purpose. MistWarp has three space types:

- **Studios** are shared project galleries.
- **Challenges** collect entries for a prompt or event.
- **Collections** organize projects you want to keep together. Collections may be public, unlisted, or private.

Space owners can invite managers, control visibility, and choose whether anyone may submit a project. Project creators can remove their own projects. Managers can remove any project from the space.

Following a space keeps it in your personal space list.

## Starter kits

The Starter kits page opens small working projects in the editor. Current kits cover platformer controls, dialogue, online scores, mobile controls, and extension development.

Change the example blocks first. The rest of the editor works like any other MistWarp project, so you can save the result to your account when it becomes your own.
