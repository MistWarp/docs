# The community site

The MistWarp community site at [mistwarp.org](https://mistwarp.org/) is where you publish projects, find other people's work and give feedback. You can use the editor without an account. You need a Rotur account to publish projects, follow people and sync your settings across devices.

- [Discovery and spaces](/community/discovery-and-spaces) covers the Explore page, spaces and starter kits.
- [Releases, previews and diagnostics](/community/releases-and-feedback) covers publishing releases, draft previews, bug reports, contributions and project teams.
- [Safety and account controls](/community/safety-and-account-controls) covers muting, blocking, reporting, and exporting or deleting your data.

To publish a project from the editor, see [project management](/editor/project-management).
